Personal Health AI

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

1. Overview

Personal Health AI is a personal health and wellness automation project designed for individual use. It integrates WHOOP health data with automated workflows and AI-powered analysis.

2. Information Collected

With the user's authorization, the application may access the following WHOOP information:

  • Sleep duration and sleep performance
  • Sleep stages and sleep timing
  • Recovery scores
  • Heart rate variability (HRV)
  • Resting heart rate
  • Daily strain and activity information
  • Workout records
  • Body measurements, when authorized

3. How Information Is Used

Authorized health information is intended to support personalized wellness insights, sleep tracking, recovery analysis, exercise planning, and daily activity recommendations.

The application is not intended to provide medical diagnoses or replace professional medical advice.

4. Third-Party Services

The planned automation uses the following services:

  • WHOOP API for authorized health data access.
  • n8n Cloud for workflow automation and processing.
  • OpenAI API for generating personalized analysis.
  • A notification provider, to be selected, for report delivery.

Health information may be transmitted to these services when the relevant integrations are enabled. Their respective privacy policies and data processing terms also apply.

5. Data Storage and Security

The application is designed to use authenticated connections and secure credential storage. Only information needed for the authorized automation should be processed or retained.

Data retention settings will be determined before the automation is activated.

6. Data Sharing

Personal Health AI is intended for individual use. Health information is not intended to be sold or shared for advertising purposes.

Authorized service providers may process information as necessary to operate the application.

7. Data Access and Deletion

The user may revoke WHOOP authorization through the available account or integration controls.

Requests to access or delete information stored by the application can be directed to the contact email below.

8. Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated when the application's integrations, data handling, or functionality change.
